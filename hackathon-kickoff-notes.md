# Hackathon

## Beacon Health
- The credentialing process is fully manual after a system transition following hospital acquisitions in Michigan.
- Previously automated credential checks, 30-day expiry alerts, and file uploads.
- Current process: a manager checks credentials for 60 associates across several public portals and prints them into physical files. Ultrasound, radiology tech, and CT credentials require separate checks.
- Scale: about 200 radiology associates and 1,000-2,000+ system-wide.
- The goal is time savings; false verification is not the concern. Microsoft Copilot is in use.

## Goodwill Michiana
- E-commerce reporting is manual and spread across Shop Goodwill, Amazon, eBay, Cash Monkey, and other platforms. Store sales reports arrive nightly, while weekend e-commerce data is delayed until Monday.
- Manual entry into Business Central adds time and error risk. Pulling reports takes an estimated 30-60 minutes daily across 24 stores.
- Desired dashboard metrics: revenue, gross/net margin, revenue per labor hour, year-over-year growth; listing production and backlog; sell-through, average/median sale price; buyer counts, repeat/new buyers, satisfaction/NPS; and sales, margin, and sell-through by category.
- E-commerce is about 5% of donated-goods retail; some Goodwills reach 10-15%. Online listings may compete with in-store resellers, while auctions can return much more for select items.
- Existing in-store AI flags items for e-commerce; an “e-commerce eye” in two stores has brought rejection close to zero.
- Hackathon scope: synthetic/public data only. Actual Goodwill data may be available after the three-month Innovation Sprint Lab.

## Defense Innovation Unit

### Track 1: Cryptographic message masking
- Problem: encrypted messages over SMS/social platforms can still look conspicuous.
- Idea: explore generative AI for dynamic masking and obfuscation of plaintext messages that can be decoded by the intended recipient.
- Framing discussed: a shared encoding/decoding scheme, such as a Pig Latin-style substitution. Software-only; no hardware required.

### Track 2: RF spectrum analysis for drone operators
- Goal: make RF spectrum concepts more accessible to operators without specialist training, using plain-language explanations and an interactive assistant.
- No SDR hardware will be on-site. Suggested research resources included KiwiSDR and SignalID Wiki. Voice integration may be worth exploring.
