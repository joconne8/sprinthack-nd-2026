# Questions for Amanda

Use these questions to guide a mentor or judge conversation about the Goodwill Michiana project. The goal is to clarify priorities, current workflows, KPI definitions, month-end close requirements, and what would make the prototype valuable to Goodwill.

## Problem Priority

1. Of the two Goodwill needs, which matters more right now: daily e-commerce visibility or month-end close automation?
2. What part of the current process causes the most pain for your team?
3. If we could eliminate one manual step this weekend, which one would create the most value?
4. What does success look like for Goodwill after this hackathon: a dashboard, an automation prototype, a data model, or a clear implementation roadmap?

## Current Workflow

5. Which reports are pulled every day versus only at month-end?
6. Which source systems are most important: ShopGoodwill, Amazon, eBay, Cash Monkey, Upright, Goodwill Books, or something else?
7. Are the reports usually CSV, Excel, PDF, email attachments, or portal-only?
8. Are report formats stable month to month, or do columns and layouts change?
9. Who currently owns the daily summary spreadsheet and month-end allocation workbook?

## KPI Definitions

10. How does Goodwill define "customer" for each marketplace?
11. Is a customer count based on buyers, orders, rows, unique emails, or something else?
12. How should revenue be defined: gross revenue, net revenue, revenue after refunds, or revenue after fees?
13. Which three KPIs would leadership care about most on a monthly dashboard?
14. For the 2027 goals, do you already have targets for net margin, revenue per labor hour, and sell-through rate?
15. How are labor hours tracked today?

## Month-End Close

16. Which month-end source is hardest to prepare: Cash Monkey, Upright, Jewelry, shipping, ShopGoodwill, Goodwill Books, eBay, or Amazon?
17. Which source most often creates reconciliation issues?
18. What are the highest-risk manual entries into Business Central?
19. Does Business Central accept CSV imports, or would an API integration be needed long-term?
20. What fields are required for a Business Central journal entry or AR invoice?
21. What would make a Business Central export credible for a prototype demo?

## Allocation Workbook

22. Is the allocation workbook considered the source of truth today?
23. Are the workbook formulas stable enough that we should reproduce them in code?
24. Which tabs or orange input fields are most important to automate first?
25. Are there control totals in the workbook that the team relies on before posting?
26. Are there any rules in the workbook that are undocumented or known only by one person?

## Exceptions and Controls

27. What should happen when a required report is missing?
28. Who owns exceptions during close?
29. What are the most common exceptions: missing files, wrong dates, duplicate files, revenue mismatches, supplier issues, or posting errors?
30. Would a dashboard showing source status and unresolved exceptions be useful even before full automation?

## Demo Guidance

31. If we demo only one workflow, should it be daily reporting or month-end close?
32. Would Amanda and Goodwill prefer a realistic finance workflow over a polished visual dashboard?
33. What sample data can we safely use for the demo?
34. Are mock Business Central outputs acceptable if we clearly show the mapping and reconciliation logic?
35. What would make you say, "Yes, this team understood our problem"?
